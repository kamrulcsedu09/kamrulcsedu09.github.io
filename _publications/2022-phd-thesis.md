This dissertation proposes mechanisms for fast and intelligent content delivery in vehicular networks based on Information-Centric Networking (ICN). Its principal contributions comprise a cluster-based core-network mechanism, a hybrid caching scheme, and artificial-intelligence-based mobility and handover prediction to support seamless content delivery to moving vehicles.
