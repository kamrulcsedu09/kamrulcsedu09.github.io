I contribute to international standardisation in the **ITU Telecommunication Standardization Sector (ITU-T)**, in collaboration with Prof. Seong Ho Jeong. My contributions concern two topics: fast message delivery for distributed ledger technology (DLT)-based services, and requirements and architecture for Information-Centric Networking (ICN)-based mobile multimedia services.

## Draft ITU-T Recommendations

| Recommendation | Title | Milestones |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ITU-T H.DLT-FMD | Framework for fast message delivery for DLT-based services | Output drafts: Geneva, Switzerland (2023); Rennes, France (2024); Geneva, Switzerland (2025) |
| ITU-T H.643.2 (F.ICNMMS) | Requirements and architecture for ICN-based mobile multimedia services | Output draft: Rennes, France (2024); consent of draft new Recommendation: Geneva, Switzerland (2025) |
