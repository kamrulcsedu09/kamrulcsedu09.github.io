My research addresses the design of intelligent, efficient and secure wireless communication networks. It combines machine learning with network architecture, with a current emphasis on AI-native sixth-generation (6G) systems and on the standardisation of agentic AI. The principal research areas are outlined below, together with representative publications.

## 1. AI-Native 6G Networks and Network Intelligence

Sixth-generation networks are expected to embed artificial intelligence throughout the network rather than treat it as an external add-on. My work examines how shared data and learning models can be used to predict network conditions and allocate resources efficiently, and how 6G performance should be evaluated in terms of key performance indicators (KPIs), tools and AI models.

**Representative publications**
- [Shared Data Intelligence-Driven Multi-Task Prediction for Efficient Resource Utilization in 6G Networks](/publication/2026-shared-data-intelligence-driven-multi-task-prediction-for-ef) — ICUFN 2026
- [Performance Evaluation and Optimization for 6G Networks: A Survey of KPIs, Tools, and AI Models](/publication/2025-performance-evaluation-and-optimization-for-6g-networks-a-su) — *ICT Express*

## 2. Agentic AI and AI Agents in 3GPP and O-RAN

AI agents that can perceive, reason and act autonomously are becoming a subject of standardisation. I study how agentic AI is being addressed in the 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP), particularly in SA2 (System Architecture) and SA5 (Management, Orchestration and Charging), and in the O-RAN Alliance. This includes the architectural, management and interoperability implications for future mobile networks.

## 3. Machine Learning for Radio Resource and Mobility Management

Accurate prediction of channel and mobility conditions allows networks to adapt proactively. My work applies supervised learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning and federated learning to channel quality indicator (CQI) prediction, throughput prediction and handover decisions in 5G and beyond-5G networks, including privacy-preserving approaches for multi-access edge computing (MEC).

## 4. Information-Centric Networking and Vehicular Communication

Information-Centric Networking (ICN) retrieves content by name rather than by host address, which suits mobile and content-heavy environments. My doctoral and subsequent research developed cluster-based ICN architectures, hybrid and proactive caching, and learning-based mobility and handover prediction to deliver content quickly and reliably to moving vehicles and Internet of Things (IoT) devices.

## 5. Blockchain, Distributed Ledger Technology and Network Security

Secure and trustworthy data exchange is essential for industrial and vehicular systems. I investigate blockchain and distributed ledger technology (DLT), including scalable and fast consensus mechanisms, together with software-defined networking (SDN)-based security architectures for the Industrial Internet of Things and Industry 4.0.
