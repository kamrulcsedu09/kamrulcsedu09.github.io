I am a Post-Doctoral Research Fellow in the **AI and Networking Lab** and a Lecturer in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering, **Hankuk University of Foreign Studies (HUFS)**, Yongin, Republic of Korea.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and wireless communication networks. My current work concentrates on **AI-native 6G systems** and on the **standardisation of agentic AI and AI agents** in the 3rd Generation Partnership Project (3GPP), with particular attention to the activities of SA2 (System Architecture) and SA5 (Management, Orchestration and Charging), and in the **O-RAN Alliance**.

I received my Ph.D. in Information and Communications Engineering from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies in 2022. My doctoral research addressed fast and intelligent content delivery in vehicular networks based on Information-Centric Networking (ICN), including cluster-based core-network mechanisms, hybrid caching strategies, and AI-based mobility and handover prediction.

Research Interests
------
- AI-native 6G networks and network intelligence
- Agentic AI and AI agents in 3GPP and O-RAN architectures
- Machine-learning-based radio resource management, including channel quality indicator (CQI) prediction
- Information-Centric Networking (ICN) and intelligent content caching
- Vehicular communication, mobility management and handover prediction
- Blockchain and distributed ledger technology (DLT) for secure networking
- Autonomous networking and network security for the Industrial Internet of Things

Teaching
------
Since March 2024, I have taught in the Department of Information and Communications Engineering at HUFS. My courses cover computer networks, network security, network simulation and performance evaluation, next-generation and AI-enabled mobile communications, multimedia communication, and algorithm design and analysis. In the current semester (Fall 2026), I teach **Multimedia Communication** and **Algorithm Design and Analysis**. A complete list of courses is available on the [Teaching](/teaching/) page.
