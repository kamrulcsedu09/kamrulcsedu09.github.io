An undergraduate course on the representation, compression and transmission of multimedia data, including image, audio and video fundamentals, colour models, and image file formats, with attention to the role of artificial intelligence in contemporary multimedia systems.

**Principal textbook:** Z.-N. Li, M. S. Drew and J. Liu, *Fundamentals of Multimedia*, 2nd ed., Springer.
